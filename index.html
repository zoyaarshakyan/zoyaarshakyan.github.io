Research Interests

Macroeconomics · Monetary Economics · Uncertainty

I am a PhD student in Economics at CERGE-EI in Prague, and a visiting PhD student at New York University during Fall 2026.

My research focuses on monetary policy, uncertainty, and the transmission of economic shocks. In my current work, I study how financial markets respond to Federal Reserve announcements when natural disasters create uncertainty about economic conditions.

I am also interested in macroeconomic models with production networks and sector-specific shocks.

Research

Disaster-Driven Uncertainty and Information Effects: Market Responses to Federal Reserve Announcements Research in progress

Preliminary and incomplete

This project investigates whether market responses to FOMC announcements depend on the economic state, with particular attention to periods following natural disasters. It examines uncertainty responses and the distinction between monetary policy surprises and information conveyed by Federal Reserve announcements.

Monetary Policy in Multi-Sector Production Networks Research in progress

Ongoing research on monetary policy design and sectoral shocks in a multi-sector New Keynesian framework.
